Send this link to clients.

The ?v= value is the share card's content hash. It changes whenever the
card changes, so WhatsApp treats it as a new URL and fetches a fresh
preview instead of reusing the one it cached (it keeps previews about a
week and offers no way to purge them). The site ignores the parameter, so
the page is identical either way.

Regenerate with: python3 build/build.py
